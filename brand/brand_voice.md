# SOVEREIGN AI — Brand Voice Guide

Version 1.0

---

## 1. The voice in one line

Direct, sovereign, precise, quietly powerful. We write like someone who owns the system and expects you to run it — not like a press release that has never touched a terminal.

---

## 2. Sounds like X, not like Y

| Context | Sounds like (X) | Not like (Y) |
|---|---|---|
| Product launch | "The Orchestrator repo is live. Clone it, run it, and your agents answer to you." | "We are thrilled to announce the launch of our groundbreaking platform!" |
| Privacy promise | "Your weights, your memory, your logs. They never leave hardware with your name on it." | "We take privacy very seriously and are committed to protecting your data." |
| Hardware | "The Ring reads presence and intent from your bio-signals. No cloud round-trip." | "Introducing the next generation of wearable technology!" |
| Pricing | "Self-host: free. Managed: what it costs us plus 20%." | "Contact our sales team for a custom enterprise quote!" |
| Setbacks | "The Buds slipped two weeks. They ship when the latency profile is right." | "We are delighted to announce our new timeline!" |

---

## 3. Forbidden words

Never use these. If one appears in a draft, delete it and say what you actually mean.

| Forbidden | Replace with |
|---|---|
| game-changer | "changes the latency profile," "removes the cloud dependency" |
| revolutionary | "first self-hosted…," "no other device does X on-device" |
| cutting-edge | "current," "shipping," the actual spec |
| in today's fast-paced world | delete the sentence; start with the fact |
| let's dive in | "Here's the plan." |
| unlock the potential | "you can now…," "here's what it does" |

Also banned: empower, seamless, robust, unprecedented, best-in-class, state-of-the-art, transformative, synergy, holistic, harness, leverage, "drives value," "solution" (as filler noun), "we're excited to," "stay tuned."

## 4. Before / after rewrites

### Example 1 — Email hero
**Before:** "In today's fast-paced world, AI is evolving at an unprecedented rate. Let's dive in and unlock the potential of your data with our cutting-edge platform!"

**After:** "Every prompt you send to a cloud model is a copy of your thinking, stored on hardware you don't own. The Sovereign Orchestrator runs the same class of model on your machine. Here's what happens when inference is local."

### Example 2 — Feature description
**Before:** "Our revolutionary BCI ring empowers users to seamlessly control their digital life, harnessing the power of neural signals in a truly transformative way."

**After:** "The Ring reads three bio-signals — heart-rate variability, skin conductance, and movement intent — and maps them to intent events. Inference is on-device. The event log is yours. Export it, delete it, or never store it."

### Example 3 — Launch announcement
**Before:** "We are thrilled to announce our groundbreaking new product line, a game-changer in the AI space that will revolutionize how you interact with technology!"

**After:** "SOVEREIGN ships four things: an orchestrator, an OS, a ring, and a pair of earbuds. All of them run on your hardware. The waitlist opens today; access opens in three tiers, VIP first."

---

## 5. Sentence-level rules

- Default to declarative sentences. State, then support.
- One idea per paragraph. One CTA per message.
- Use numbers instead of superlatives: "3 ms on-device," not "blazing fast."
- Address the user as owner: "your model," "your hardware," "your rules."
- Never beg ("Don't miss out!") and never brag ("We're the best!"). State capability and let it stand.
- Machine speech (logs, specs, CLI output) is terse and factual. Human speech (emails, posts) is direct and calm.

---

## 6. The mission test

Before any copy ships, ask: **Does this sentence return control to the user?** If the answer is no, rewrite it. This single test keeps every surface on voice, from a GitHub README to a billboard.
