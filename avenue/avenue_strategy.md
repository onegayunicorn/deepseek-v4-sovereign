# SOVEREIGN AI — Go-to-Market Avenues

Version 1.0

---

## Avenue 1 — Developer-first open-source

**What ships:** Sovereign Orchestrator monorepo (open core), Sovereign OS developer builds, one-click self-host demo on HuggingFace Spaces.

**ICP:** Self-hosting AI developers — ML engineers, homelab operators, indie hackers, platform teams already running DeepSeek/Llama/Qwen locally and frustrated that orchestration assumes cloud.

**Channels:** GitHub, HuggingFace Spaces, X, Hacker News, r/LocalLLaMA, Discord, developer conferences.

**Offer:** Free self-host, forever, no account required. Quickstart that goes from clone to running agents in under 30 minutes. Spaces demo for the "try before you clone" crowd. Paid managed tier as convenience only.

**90-day goals:**
- 5,000 GitHub stars on the Orchestrator monorepo
- 2,000 waitlist signups attributed to the repo and Spaces demo
- 200 live Spaces deployments
- 15,000 community members across Discord and GitHub discussions

**KPIs:** Stars, clones, Spaces deploys, waitlist signups, time-to-first-run (<30 min), community retention (weekly active in Discord).

---

## Avenue 2 — Health-tech wellness DTC

**What ships:** Sovereign Ring (BCI neural ring) and Sovereign Buds (AI earbuds), DTC store, waitlist with tiered access.

**ICP:** Quantified-self users and neurotech early adopters, 25–45, urban, privacy-sensitive, already wearing rings, bands, or earbuds — and increasingly refusing to sync biometrics to a brand's cloud.

**Channels:** Xiaohongshu (CES-optimized posts), TikTok, wellness newsletters, health-tech creators, DTC storefront, Product Hunt.

**Offer:** Waitlist with VIP / waitlist / public tiered access. Launch pricing on Ring and Buds. "First 100 buyers" exclusive bundle to accelerate early conversion.

**90-day goals:**
- 8,000 waitlist signups
- 1,200 units sold across Ring and Buds
- 15 creator reviews published (embargoed to launch window)
- CAC under $45 per order

**KPIs:** Waitlist size, opt-in rate, CTR on teaser creative, sell-through rate, day-1 revenue multiple, review rate (2–8% benchmark), CAC.

---

## Avenue 3 — Enterprise privacy B2B

**What ships:** Sovereign deployments — Orchestrator + OS on customer hardware, SLA, compliance packs (data residency, audit logs, access controls).

**ICP:** CISOs, data-protection officers, and platform leads in regulated industries — health, finance, legal, public sector — where cloud AI is disqualified for sensitive workloads.

**Channels:** Direct sales, security and compliance conferences, partner/channel (managed service providers), whitepapers and compliance guides, warm intros from open-source users inside target accounts.

**Offer:** 30-day self-host pilot with compliance pack included. Deployment runs on customer hardware or their private cloud. Subscription with SLA. No data ever touches our infrastructure.

**90-day goals:**
- 25 qualified pipeline opportunities
- 3 pilot deployments signed
- 1 reference customer case study published

**KPIs:** Pipeline value, demo requests, pilot sign rate, time-to-deploy, pilot-to-paid conversion, churn.

---

## Cross-avenue sequencing

- **T-21 to T-1:** Avenues 1 and 2 drive waitlist; Avenue 3 runs quiet pre-briefs (no public noise).
- **T-0:** All three fire together — repo live, DTC open, enterprise pilot page live.
- **T+1 to T+30:** Avenue 1 feeds credibility to Avenue 3 (users inside target accounts become warm intros); Avenue 2 feeds social proof to the whole brand.
